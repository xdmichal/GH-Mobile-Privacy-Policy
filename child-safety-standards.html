GH Mobile – Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 27 October 2026

GH Mobile is an unofficial, community-made Android client for the GTANet IRC network and the #gta2gh channel (GTA2 Game Hunter community). It is intended for users aged 16 and over. This page describes our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Zero tolerance

We do not allow child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualisation of minors, sextortion, trafficking of minors, or any other form of child sexual abuse and exploitation, in the app or by means of the app. The app is not designed for or directed at children and is not intended for users under 16.

2. How the service works

The app is only a client. The developer does not run the IRC network or the channel and does not store or host users’ messages on any server. Messages travel from the user’s phone to the IRC server (GTANet) and are visible to other users of the network. The app stores chat history only on the user’s own device.

3. Reporting concerns

  • In the app: users can block (ignore) any user, which hides that user’s messages on their device.
  • Network and channel operators: content on the IRC network is moderated by the operators of #gta2gh and of GTANet. Users can message them, or use the network’s own abuse contacts, to have a user removed from the channel or the network.
  • Developer contact: concerns about child safety can also be reported to the developer by e-mail: xdmichal@gmail.com.

4. How we respond

Reports about CSAE are treated as the highest priority. We review them without delay, we ask the network and channel operators to remove the user, and, where required or appropriate, we report to the competent authorities, such as national child-protection hotlines and law enforcement (in Poland, for example, Dyżurnet.pl and the police), and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable. We do not tell users to keep suspected CSAM; we ask them not to copy or forward it.

5. Legal compliance

We comply with the child-safety laws that apply to us and cooperate with the competent authorities. The developer’s designated contact person for child-safety matters is available at the e-mail address above and is prepared to discuss our CSAE policies and how they are enforced.
